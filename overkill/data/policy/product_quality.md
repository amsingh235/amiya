## product_quality-definition
Product quality: definition and qualifying conditions. The goods arrived but were damaged, defective, counterfeit, used when sold as new, the wrong item, size or model, or materially different from the description, and the merchant has not agreed to a refund. Qualifying conditions: (1) the cardholder received the goods; (2) a specific defect or mismatch with the description or order; (3) the cardholder tried to resolve it, or the merchant refused a return. Network references: Visa 13.3 and 13.4, Mastercard 4853.

## product_quality-evidence
Product quality: evidence and exclusions. Evidence: photos, the original listing, an expert opinion for counterfeits, the merchant's refusal of a return. Exclusions: the item never arrived or parts of the order are missing (product_not_received, R7); the merchant accepted a return or promised a refund that never came (credit_not_processed, R2); a merchant that only offered to "look into it" has not agreed a refund, so the dispute stays product_quality; changing one's mind without any defect does not qualify.

## product_quality-precedent-1
Precedent: wool coat that was polyester. A cardholder ordered a coat listed as 100% wool; the sewn-in label said 80% polyester. The seller refused a return, citing a final-sale policy. Decision: product_quality (not as described).

## product_quality-precedent-2
Precedent: bicycle with a bent wheel. A bicycle arrived with a bent rear wheel although the courier's photo showed an intact box. The merchant denied the claim. Decision: product_quality. The goods were received; the problem is their condition (R7).
