## credit_not_processed-definition
Credit not processed: definition and qualifying conditions. The merchant acknowledged that money is owed back and the credit did not arrive, or arrived short. Qualifying conditions: (1) a merchant acknowledgement: refund confirmation or reference, accepted return, cancellation with refund wording, a promise in chat or email; (2) a reasonable time has passed since the acknowledgement, typically 7 to 15 business days; (3) no credit, or a partial credit (R8). Network references: Visa 13.6, Mastercard 4853.

## credit_not_processed-evidence
Credit not processed: evidence and exclusions. Evidence: the refund confirmation, return tracking showing delivery to the merchant, a cancellation confirmation that mentions a refund, a credit slip. Exclusions: the merchant never agreed to refund (classify the underlying problem instead: product_quality, product_not_received or service_not_rendered); recurring billing after cancellation takes precedence even when a refund was promised (cancelled_recurring, R3).

## credit_not_processed-precedent-1
Precedent: sofa returned, refund initiated, nothing posted. A cardholder returned a sofa; the courier's proof of delivery shows the retailer's warehouse received it on 2 March, and the retailer emailed "refund initiated". By 10 April no credit had posted. Decision: credit_not_processed.

## credit_not_processed-precedent-2
Precedent: airline refunded half. After cancelling a flight, an airline's app showed "refund processed: ₹18,200", but only ₹9,100 reached the card. Decision: credit_not_processed. A short credit is still a credit not processed (R8), and the refund acknowledgement outranks service_not_rendered (R2).
