## incorrect_amount-definition
Incorrect amount: definition and qualifying conditions. The cardholder agreed to the purchase but was charged more than the agreed price: an overcharge, an undisclosed fee, an altered tip, extra nights or days, the wrong quantity, or a quote that was exceeded. Qualifying conditions: (1) the cardholder recognises and agreed to the transaction; (2) there is an agreed price, such as a receipt, quote, menu, meter or booking; (3) the charged amount is higher than that price. Network references: Visa 12.5, Mastercard 4831.

## incorrect_amount-evidence
Incorrect amount: evidence and exclusions. Evidence: the receipt or quote, the statement amount, the booking terms showing which fees were disclosed. Exclusions: the price was right but the mechanics were wrong, such as currency, wrong card or late posting (processing_error, R5); one purchase charged twice for the full amount each time (duplicate_processing); a merchant the cardholder does not recognise (unauthorised); a refund smaller than promised (credit_not_processed, R8).

## incorrect_amount-precedent-1
Precedent: restaurant tip altered. A restaurant bill was ₹2,400 and the cardholder wrote a ₹200 tip on the slip, but the statement shows ₹3,400. Decision: incorrect_amount (altered tip).

## incorrect_amount-precedent-2
Precedent: undisclosed cleaning fee at a car-rental desk. A car-rental desk added ₹4,500 for "premium cleaning", a fee that was not in the booking terms. Decision: incorrect_amount, because an undisclosed fee exceeds the agreed price (R5).
