## processing_error-definition
Processing error: definition and qualifying conditions. The price was right but the transaction was processed wrongly: the wrong currency or a conversion the cardholder declined, posted to the wrong card or account, charged although the terminal showed a decline, posted months after the purchase (late presentment), or a refund processed as a charge. Qualifying conditions: (1) the cardholder agreed to the purchase and its price; (2) a technical or processing fault changed how it was charged. Network references: Visa 12.1 to 12.4 and 12.7, Mastercard 4834.

## processing_error-evidence
Processing error: evidence and exclusions. Evidence: the receipt in the original currency, the terminal's decline slip, the purchase date versus the posting date, statements from both cards involved. Exclusions: a higher price than agreed (incorrect_amount, R5); a decline followed by payment with another method, after which both were charged (duplicate_processing, R4); a refund that never arrived (credit_not_processed).

## processing_error-precedent-1
Precedent: Dubai hotel converted to rupees. A hotel in Dubai agreed a room rate in dirhams but charged the card in rupees, at a conversion rate the cardholder had declined on the terminal. Decision: processing_error (unrequested conversion, R5).

## processing_error-precedent-2
Precedent: refund keyed as a sale. At an electronics store a ₹1,500 refund was keyed as a ₹1,500 sale, so the cardholder was charged instead of credited. Decision: processing_error (incorrect transaction code).
