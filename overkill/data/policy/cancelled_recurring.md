## cancelled_recurring-definition
Cancelled recurring: definition and qualifying conditions. A recurring charge (subscription, membership, plan, auto-renewal, standing card authority) was billed after the cardholder cancelled, after the merchant confirmed the cancellation, or after the cardholder withdrew permission for future charges. Qualifying conditions: (1) a recurring arrangement the cardholder had set up; (2) cancellation or revoked consent before the disputed billing date; (3) the merchant billed again. Network references: Visa 13.2, Mastercard 4841.

## cancelled_recurring-evidence
Cancelled recurring: evidence and exclusions. Evidence: the cancellation confirmation or its date, a screenshot of the closed account, the renewal charge date. Exclusions: a one-off purchase is not recurring; a renewal the cardholder never tried to cancel is not this category; recurring charges from a merchant the cardholder never had an account with are unauthorised. A renewal after cancellation stays in this category when the merchant promised a refund (R3) or when the cardholder calls it a duplicate (R10).

## cancelled_recurring-precedent-1
Precedent: meal-kit plan cancelled in the app. A cardholder cancelled a meal-kit plan in the app on 28 May and received a confirmation email. A ₹2,999 charge for the June box posted on 1 June. Decision: cancelled_recurring.

## cancelled_recurring-precedent-2
Precedent: annual cloud-storage renewal with a refund promise. A cardholder cancelled a cloud-storage plan, the merchant billed the annual renewal anyway, and support promised to refund it within a week, which never happened. Decision: cancelled_recurring. Recurring billing after cancellation takes precedence over credit_not_processed (R3).
