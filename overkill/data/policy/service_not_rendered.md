## service_not_rendered-definition
Service not rendered: definition and qualifying conditions. The cardholder paid for a service, booking, event, class, online course, installation, repair, or travel that did not take place or was not provided as agreed, and the merchant has not agreed to refund. Qualifying conditions: (1) a purchase of a service rather than goods (R6); (2) the service date has passed, or the merchant has stopped trading or cannot perform; (3) the cardholder was willing and able to receive the service. Network references: Visa 13.1 (services), Mastercard 4855.

## service_not_rendered-evidence
Service not rendered: evidence and exclusions. Evidence: the booking confirmation, proof the cardholder turned up or was available, a cancellation notice from the merchant, an unreachable provider. Exclusions: goods, including digital goods (product_not_received, R6); a merchant that cancelled and promised a refund which never came (credit_not_processed, R2); a cardholder who simply did not attend.

## service_not_rendered-precedent-1
Precedent: wedding photographer no-show. A cardholder prepaid ₹6,000 to a wedding photographer who never arrived on the day and stopped answering calls. Decision: service_not_rendered.

## service_not_rendered-precedent-2
Precedent: bootcamp portal never unlocked. An online coding bootcamp took payment but the course portal never unlocked, and support tickets went unanswered. Decision: service_not_rendered. Course access is a service (R6), and no refund was agreed (R9).
