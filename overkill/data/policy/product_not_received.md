## product_not_received-definition
Product not received: definition and qualifying conditions. The cardholder paid for goods that never arrived, arrived only in part, or did not arrive by the agreed date, and the merchant has not agreed to a refund. Qualifying conditions: (1) a purchase of goods, physical or digital: downloads, licence keys, gift cards and in-game items count as goods (R6); (2) the expected delivery date has passed, or the merchant cannot show delivery to the cardholder; (3) the cardholder did not receive the goods, or some items were missing (R7). Network references: Visa 13.1, Mastercard 4855.

## product_not_received-evidence
Product not received: evidence and exclusions. Evidence: the order confirmation with the promised date, tracking that never shows delivery or shows delivery to the wrong place, unanswered messages to the merchant. Exclusions: goods that arrived damaged, wrong or different from the description (product_quality, R7); a booking or service that did not happen (service_not_rendered, R6); a merchant that agreed to refund and did not pay (credit_not_processed, R2).

## product_not_received-precedent-1
Precedent: smartwatch stuck at label created. A cardholder bought a smartwatch with an estimated delivery date of 5 June. Tracking stayed at "label created" from 1 June, and the seller stopped replying. Decision: product_not_received.

## product_not_received-precedent-2
Precedent: game licence key never emailed. A cardholder bought a licence key for a video game; the key was never emailed and the store's support ticket stayed unanswered. Decision: product_not_received, because digital goods count as goods (R6).
