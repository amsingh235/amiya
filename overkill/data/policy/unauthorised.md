## unauthorised-definition
Unauthorised: definition and qualifying conditions. The cardholder did not make, permit or take part in the transaction. Typical causes are a lost or stolen card, card details used online without permission (card-not-present fraud), or account takeover. Qualifying conditions: (1) the cardholder does not recognise the merchant or the purchase; (2) they did not give the card or credentials to whoever used them; (3) they received no goods or services from the transaction. If the cardholder recognises the merchant and engaged with them, use another category (R1). Network references: Visa 10.4, Mastercard 4837.

## unauthorised-evidence
Unauthorised: evidence and exclusions. Evidence: the cardholder's statement that they did not participate; card status (reported lost or stolen, or still in their possession); a delivery address, device or location that does not match the cardholder. Exclusions: a recognised merchant charging the wrong amount (incorrect_amount); a recognised subscription that renewed after cancellation (cancelled_recurring); repeated charges from a merchant the cardholder never dealt with are still unauthorised, not duplicate or recurring, because there was no purchase or plan of theirs to duplicate or cancel.

## unauthorised-precedent-1
Precedent: three overnight charges from a Romanian electronics site. A cardholder in Pune reported three charges from an electronics web shop in Romania placed within an hour, while the card stayed in their drawer. They had never visited the site, and the orders shipped to a freight forwarder. Decision: unauthorised. The merchant was unknown, the card was not shared, and nothing was received.

## unauthorised-precedent-2
Precedent: "fraud" that was a forgotten membership. A cardholder flagged a FITZONE debit as fraud. On review they had been a member and had cancelled in writing two months earlier, and the debit was the regular monthly fee. Decision: cancelled_recurring, not unauthorised. The cardholder recognised the merchant once reminded (R1), and the charge came after a cancellation (R3).
